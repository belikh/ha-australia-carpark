Carpark Australia is a Home Assistant integration that counts parking spaces across the area you choose and exposes a single set of roll-up sensors. Each sensor's attributes list the car parks in scope, how many spaces are free or occupied at each, and any pricing information the source publishes.

Install it from HACS, choose your data sources and your area, then ask "how many parking spaces are free near home?" without reading hundreds of sensors.
